# Displacement, strain, stress, and reaction forces

This theory chapter provides a short conceptual explanation for static analysis in ESD. It is intended to help users understand the meaning of the simulation inputs, solver behavior, and result quantities before using the related ESD screens.

## What does this chapter explain?

This chapter explains the main result quantities produced in static structural analysis: displacement, strain, stress, and reaction forces.

## How are these quantities related?

ESD first solves for displacement. Strain is calculated from the displacement field, stress is calculated from strain and material properties, and reaction forces are obtained at constrained regions.

## Why should users understand these results?

Understanding the relationship between these quantities helps users interpret whether a simulation result is realistic, whether the structure is deforming as expected, and where high stress regions may occur.

## What should users check?

Users should review the deformation shape, maximum displacement, stress distribution, strain pattern, and reaction force balance before accepting a result.

## What should users understand after this chapter?

After completing this chapter, users should be able to connect the theory concept with the corresponding ESD setup or result screen and use the concept to make better simulation decisions.
