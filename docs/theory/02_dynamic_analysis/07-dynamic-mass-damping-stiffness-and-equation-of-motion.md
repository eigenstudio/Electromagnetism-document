# Mass, damping, stiffness, and equation of motion

This theory chapter provides a short conceptual explanation for dynamic analysis in ESD. It is intended to help users understand the meaning of the simulation inputs, solver behavior, and result quantities before using the related ESD screens.

## What does this chapter explain?

This chapter explains the main physical quantities that control dynamic structural response: mass, damping, and stiffness.

## What is the equation of motion?

The equation of motion represents the balance between inertia forces, damping forces, internal elastic forces, and externally applied dynamic loads.

## Why are mass and damping important?

Mass controls inertia and affects natural frequencies, while damping controls how quickly vibration reduces over time. Both are essential for realistic dynamic response prediction.

## What should users check in ESD?

Users should ensure that density is defined correctly, damping assumptions are appropriate, and stiffness-related inputs such as material properties, mesh, and constraints are physically meaningful.

## What should users understand after this chapter?

After completing this chapter, users should be able to connect the theory concept with the corresponding ESD setup or result screen and use the concept to make better simulation decisions.
